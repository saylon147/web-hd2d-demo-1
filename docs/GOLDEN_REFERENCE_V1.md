## Main scene
A miniature Mediterranean-inspired rocky island with a cream cottage, terracotta pitched roof, green door/shutters, climbing vines, flower beds, stone terrace, broad garden, trees, stone steps, sandy shore, wooden pier, moored rowboat, transparent turquoise water and scattered coastal rocks. Warm, painterly, richly layered but legible at the M1 fixed elevated perspective camera.

## Revised building concept
- Exterior appears **single-storey**; basement is concealed within elevated rock mass.
- Ground floor: compact living/dining, kitchen, bedroom, bathroom; rational internal circulation.
- Basement: storage, workshop, reading/rest area; interior stairs connect to ground floor.
- Roof removable/hidden for interior cutaway; basement visible only in basement inspection mode.
- Concept-art cutaways may be spatially inconsistent. Reconcile them in M2.1 rather than copying impossible geometry.

## Island composition
- Effective walkable land ~9× cottage footprint, not 9× cottage width.
- Cottage slightly off center, terrace/flowers on approach, garden/trees behind, steps descend toward beach and pier.
- Rocky perimeter, sea visible around island, deliberate negative space and clear navigable paths.
- Maintain recognizable silhouette in the default camera view.

## Art characteristics
Soft painterly albedo, broad warm/cool value groups, mildly exaggerated architectural proportions, hand-shaped stones and foliage masses. Avoid photoreal textures, excessive uniform noise, flat low-poly placeholders masquerading as final art, harsh outlines and global pixelation.
